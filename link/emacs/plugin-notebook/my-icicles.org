Icicles is a text completion framework for Emacs, and quite an old one at that.
Icicles was superceded by Helm, then Ivy, and then those frameworks were
superceded yet again by the modern slate of =completing-read= based completion
systems such as Selectrum and Vertico.

#+begin_src emacs-lisp
  (use-package icicles
    ;; Currently disabled in favor of vertico
    :disabled)
#+end_src
